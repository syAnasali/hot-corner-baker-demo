ABOUT
The Provenance Hot Corner Story & Philosophy

Bred in Rajasthan, Fired in Stone, Printed in Rigor.

Hot Corner was conceived in Gumanpura, Kota in 2024 as an antidote to industrial fast-baking and hurried commercial espresso. We operate like an artisanal print broadsheet: every batch has an edition ledger, every flour has a traceable pedigree, and every coffee lot is roasted to preserve its living terroir.

I. THE LIVING MOTHER

‘Rukmini’ (Yr 4)

Never commercial yeast. 18 to 72 hours of cold lactic fermentation unlocks open cellular honeycomb crumb and effortless digestibility.

II. ANCIENT DURUM GRAINS

Stone-Milled Sehore

Locally milled wheat retains alive germ oils. Combined with quartz-filtered Chambal water balanced to 135 TDS for vivid flavor depth.

III. ARCHITECTURAL CALM

Lime-Wash & Teak

Crushed quartz terrazzo and breathable Rajasthani earthen plaster. A neighborhood sanctuary for morning reading and slow conversation.

THE FOUNDER'S CONVICTION
