## Brand & Style
The brand voice combines high-craft heritage baking with avant-garde editorial publishing. Rooted in tactile traditions yet executed with high-fashion magazine confidence, the aesthetic balances warm, hospitable indulgence with sharp architectural precision. 

The design language draws directly from high-end editorial print culture, Swiss typography discipline, and contemporary bistro curation:
- **Editorial Postering:** Dramatic typographic tension between high-contrast transitional serifs and clean, contemporary geometric body text.
- **Warm Architectural Rigor:** Asymmetrical grid placements, hair-thin biscuit rule lines, and purposeful whitespace evoke printed broadsheets and artisanal menu placards.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Soft warm cream surfaces layered against intense espresso ink and scorched terracotta accents provide warmth without slipping into rustic kitsch.

## Colors
The color palette draws from the oven and the broadsheet press:

- **Primary (`#D95D39` - Burnt Orange):** Represents hearth heat, maillard browning, and visual accentuation. Used for key conversion moments, editorial badges, and structural callouts.
- **Secondary (`#1C1512` - Deep Espresso):** The primary ink color. Delivers decisive contrast without the harsh, sterile chill of pure digital black. Used for display headlines, heavy dividers, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Tertiary (`#5C242C` - Muted Burgundy):** Adds rich, wine-poached depth. Reserved for seasonal editorial highlights, subtle typographic counters, and prestige badges.
- **Neutral Background (`#FBF7F0` - Warm Cream) & Neutral Surface (`#F4EFE6` - Baked Flour):** Replaces clinical digital white with the tactile grain of untreated book paper and unbleached pastry parchment.
- **Border / Rule Neutral (`#E3DAC9` - Soft Biscuit):** Used for hair-thin structural borders and delicate column separators, framing content cleanly against cream backdrops.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is deliberate and dramatic:
- **Playfair Display** commands presence at large scales. It injects classical transitional editorial flair, evocative of traditional bakeries and print broadsheets. It should always be set with tight leading and subtle negative letter spacing to feel intentional and crafted.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** grounds the system with geometric balance, generous counters, and effortless legibility across digital screens. It is used for all operational content, pricing, menus, and narrative passages.
- **The Micro-Caps Convention (`label-caps`):** Technical details, batch timestamps, location tags, and item categories are set in bold, uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with wide letter spacing (`0.14em`) to mirror archival labeling and print metadata.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows an asymmetric 12-column broadsheet grid structure designed to accommodate oversized editorial displays alongside functional e-commerce interactions.

- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements break symmetry—hero typography spans 7 to 9 columns, with adjacent tasting notes or pricing tables pinned to the remaining 3 to 5 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Typographic displays scale gracefully to preserve editorial impact without awkward line wraps.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer gutters. Editorial elements flow linearly, turning horizontal editorial spreads into vertical stacked cards separated by fine rule lines.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing relies on stark contrasts—dense, compact metadata tables juxtaposed against expansive `4xl` vertical buffers surrounding featured items and imagery.

## Elevation & Depth
In keeping with print broadsheets and modernist menus, the design system avoids heavy drop shadows and fuzzy ambient lighting. Depth is articulated purely through tonal layers, crisp borders, and deliberate overlap:

- **Base Layer:** `#FBF7F0` provides the overall page atmosphere.
- **Surface Layer:** Secondary content containers, inspection drawers, and sidebars utilize `#F4EFE6` with crisp `1px solid #E3DAC9` dividers.
- **Elevated Interactive Overlays:** Modals, toast notifications, and floating order bars utilize solid `#1C1512` surfaces with contrasting `#FBF7F0` typography. When absolute physical separation from content is required, apply a crisp offset shadow: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #1C1512` over a warm white card, preserving tactile print tactility rather than artificial soft blur.

## Shapes
The design system embraces the "Soft" geometry philosophy (level `1`):
- Standard components (buttons, tags, input surfaces) maintain subtle `0.25rem` (4px) rounded corners. This removes harsh digital razor edges while maintaining the geometric discipline of cut paper and printed tickets.
- Medium containers and card frames scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Badges and status pills intentionally diverge to full pill radii (`9999px`) when used as editorial stickers or seasonal roundels, creating a focal punch against rectangular editorial columns.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#D95D39`, label `#FBF7F0` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600, `0.25rem` radius, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#C54E2C` with no elevation shift.
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Background transparent, border `1px solid #1C1512`, label `#1C1512`. Hover inverts to solid `#1C1512` fill with `#FBF7F0` text.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, `#D95D39` uppercase tracking with an understated trailing arrow (`→`) offset by `0.25rem`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** `#F4EFE6` fill, `1px solid #E3DAC9` border, `11px` uppercase tracking in deep espresso (`#1C1512`), `0.25rem` radius.
- **Prestige Badge (Oven Special / Batch):** Background `#5C242C`, text `#FBF7F0`, rounded pill (`9999px`), `0.25rem 0.65rem` padding.

### Cards & Menu Listings
- **Editorial Menu Card:** Background `#FBF7F0` or `#F4EFE6`, framed by a fine `1px solid #E3DAC9` perimeter. High-contrast Playfair Display header at top, ingredient notes below in body-md, with price pinned in bold Plus Jakarta Sans right-aligned along a dotted baseline leader.
- **Hero Feature Card:** Asymmetric border treatments, deep espresso header ribbon, warm parchment body container.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp `#FBF7F0` surface with `1px solid #E3DAC9` frame, `0.25rem` corner radius. Focused state shifts border to `1px solid #D95D39` with no outer glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal geometric frames with `#1C1512` borders and `#D95D39` active fill, ensuring clear tactile feedback.

### Lists & Tables
- **Bakery Schedule / Daily Bakes:** Uncluttered row layouts separated by `1px solid #E3DAC9`. Time tags use `label-caps` in deep espresso, product names in `headline-md`, and live inventory status indicated via warm burnt orange typography.
